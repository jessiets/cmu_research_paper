| P20 | 2026-11-11 | Methods (shared preprocessing) | Describe shared side extraction. All methods use one extractor. It traces the largest external contour of each piece's alpha mask at 2× resolution, takes the four corners as the extrema of x + y and x − y, splits the contour into four clockwise sides, resamples each side to 96 points by arc length, and marks a side flat when its deviation from the corner-to-corner chord is below 5% of the derived pitch. Methods derive pitch from side lengths and are never given the grid size. The 2× tracing keeps the two halves of a cut within about 0.5 px of each other, so the extractor does not systematically penalize shape-based matching. | D8, spec B5 | Done |
| P21 | 2026-11-11 | Methods (evidence measures) | Describe GEO and COLOR as implemented. **GEO:** each side's 96 points minus its chord midpoint give a profile. A relation's score is the negative mean L1 distance between the two facing profiles, with the facing side reversed. GEO never reads pixels. **COLOR:** each side is sampled 1 px inward along its normal, in CIELAB, with bilinear interpolation weighted by the piece mask so transparent pixels never mix in. A relation's score is the negative mean squared CIELAB difference between facing samples, following the form of Cho, Avidan, and Freeman (2010), which the paper already cites. Both use the shared side extraction, and excluded (flat or failed) sides get the worst score. | Spec B7 | Done |
